Setup — your reference clip

Read a script out loud, in a quiet room, mic 15–30 cm away, in your normal voice. The recording becomes the voice every backend clones from. Pick the script below — the transcript stored with the clip is the exact text you read, so the backends that need a transcript get it automatically.

Why the script matters, and how long the clip should be. The backends clone from a short prompt — about 10 seconds for Chatterbox, Qwen3 and CosyVoice, up to 30 seconds for VoxCPM2. Read the whole passage, then use the cleanest stretch: a longer recording is material to trim from, not extra voice data the model hears in full. The transcript saved with the clip is the full passage — if you trim the clip afterwards, update the matching refs/<name>.txt to the exact words in the trimmed clip.
